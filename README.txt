EV Charging Predictor learns when your car leaves, when it comes home and how much energy it needs, just from your EV charger. Pair it with your existing charger and it picks up your weekly routine from when the cable is plugged in and out. It runs entirely on your Homey: it only reads your charger, and nothing leaves your home.

Use the predictions in your own Flows, for example to make sure the car is charged by the time you leave, or to skip a charging plan when it barely needs a top-up. Easee chargers give real predictions from day one; other chargers learn in one to two weeks.
